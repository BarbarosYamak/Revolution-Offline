Every live bot that fights carries at least 100 bandages (owner, 2026-09-05, watching the wave).

**How to apply:** treat 100 as the floor for hunters, scale the target up with wealth/plan per [[thresholds-must-be-dynamic]]; never lower it to make a smoke pass. Non-fighters (scribe, pure crafters) keep their own smaller numbers.
