# M3.6 -- charge a Runebook with a Recall scroll.
#
# RUN ONLY AS SESSION 7 (RevolutionMageGM), the fenced mechanics probe.
#
# Historical rule, OFFICIAL_REVOLUTION_UPDATE 13.05.2009:
#   "Sarj (recall scroll ekleyerek) ile runebook kullanirsaniz magery
#    yetenegine ihtiyaciniz olmayacaktir."
#   "If you use the runebook with charges (by adding recall scrolls) you will
#    not need the magery skill."
#
# So: adding a Recall scroll adds a charge, and a charged use does not require
# Magery. The RATE (one scroll = one charge) and the absence of a maximum are
# reconstruction -- the archive states neither -- and are marked as such in
# revolution_runebook.scp.

wait_world
sleep 2500
ensure_peace

remember pack backpack
open @pack
wait_action
sleep 1500

remember book pack_graphic 0x22C5
require book
remember scroll pack_graphic 0x1F4C
require scroll

use @book
wait_action
sleep 2500
gump_report

# Button 40 = "add a Recall scroll as a charge".
gump_button 40
sleep 2000
wait_target
target @scroll
sleep 3000

use @book
wait_action
sleep 2500
gump_report

say the book is charged
sleep 1500
logout
