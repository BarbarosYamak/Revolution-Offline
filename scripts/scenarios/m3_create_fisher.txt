# M3 -- create the income character the ordinary way.
#
# The 0x00 create-character packet asks for three skills; Source-X clamps each
# to 50 and the sum to 100 in CChar::InitPlayer, and the shard's own newbie
# templates (templates_special/sp_tm_newbie.scp) decide the kit. Nothing is
# granted here -- the client asks and the server answers.
#
# Fishing 50 / Mining 30 / Blacksmithy 20: a hybrid gatherer, not a profession.
# The same character can fish for coin, mine its own ore and smith some of it,
# which is the shape M3 is meant to support.

wait_world
sleep 3000
request_skills
sleep 2000
skill_report
remember pack backpack
open @pack
wait_action
say fisher reporting for work
sleep 1000
logout
