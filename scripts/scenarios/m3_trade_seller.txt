# M3 secure trade -- the seller's half.
#
# A trade is OPENED by dropping an item on the other player: that is the only
# way a 2.0.x client has (Source-X CClient::Event_Item_Drop ->
# Cmd_SecureTrade). So the first fish is both the offer and the request.
#
# The seller waits for the buyer's coins to appear before accepting, and the
# buyer waits for the fish before paying, so neither side can be left holding
# an accepted-but-empty window and neither needs a timer.

wait_world
sleep 2000
ensure_peace

travel_point 1453 1765 1
wait_travel
expect_travel ok

remember pack backpack
open @pack
wait_action
mark_gold
mark_item 0x09CC,0x09CD,0x09CE,0x09CF

scan_mobiles
sleep 4000
remember buyer mobile_name RevolutionMedic
require buyer

remember fish pack_graphic 0x09CC
require fish

# Dropping the fish on them IS the trade request.
trade_start @buyer @fish
wait_action
expect success
wait_trade_open
wait_trade_mine 1

# Their coin before our signature.
wait_trade_offer 1
trade_accept

wait_trade_closed
expect_trade completed

sleep 2500
open @pack
wait_action
sleep 2000
expect_gold_gain
expect_item_drop 0x09CC,0x09CD,0x09CE,0x09CF

say sold
sleep 1000
logout
