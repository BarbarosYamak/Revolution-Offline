# M3.5 -- prove NPC Teaching live, and prove the price.
#
# Sphere's Teaching system is stock and real: CChar::NPC_OnTrainHear
# (Source-X CCharNPCAct_Vendor.cpp:370) answers the speech verb TRAIN plus a
# skill key, quotes a price and remembers the offer; handing over gold
# completes it (NPC_OnTrainPay, :273).
#
# What this runtime will teach, from its own sphere.ini rather than from
# folklore:
#
#   NPCTrainPercent=30   -> up to 30% of the TRAINER's own skill
#   NPCTrainMax=420      -> never above 42.0, whatever the trainer knows
#   NPCTrainCost=1       -> 1 gold per 0.1 skill
#
# So a GM trainer teaches to 30.0, and 0 -> 30.0 costs exactly 300 gold --
# which is the project owner's memory of Revolution ("about 30.0, around
# 300gp") reproduced to the digit by the stock defaults.
#
# The test: RevolutionFisher has Arms Lore 2.6. A blacksmith carries
# ARMSLORE={50.0 75.0} (npcs/c_vendor_human.scp), so 30% of that is 15.0-22.5
# and the trainer should be able to sell real points. Expected cost is the
# point delta in tenths, one gold each.
#
# Proof is the server's numbers before and after -- skill AND gold -- not the
# fact that a packet was sent.

wait_world
sleep 2000
ensure_peace

request_skills
sleep 1500
skill_report

remember pack backpack
open @pack
wait_action
mark_gold
mark_skill 4

travel_service blacksmith
wait_travel
expect_travel ok

scan_mobiles
sleep 4000
remember smith mobile_trade blacksmith
require smith

travel_entity @smith 1
wait_travel

# An NPC ignores a keyword until it is in conversation (M3, e_Human_HearUnk),
# and CanTouch is two tiles against a vendor that wanders.
sleep 4000
say hello
sleep 3000

travel_entity @smith 1
wait_travel
npc_train @smith ArmsLore
wait_action
expect success
sleep 1500

# Pay. Gold is coin, so this is an ordinary lift-and-drop onto the trainer.
remember gold pack_graphic 0x0EED
require gold
travel_entity @smith 1
wait_travel
give @smith @gold 250
sleep 5000

request_skills
sleep 2000
skill_report

open @pack
wait_action
sleep 2000

say the smith taught me something
sleep 1000
logout
