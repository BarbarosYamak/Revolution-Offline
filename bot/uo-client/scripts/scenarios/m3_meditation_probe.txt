# M3 -- how fast does mana actually come back?
#
# The first training block measured 80 s to regain 4 mana: a flat 1 point per
# 20 s, which is exactly sphere.ini's Regen1=20 and, per
# CChar::Stats_GetRegenVal, is independent of INT. At that rate Magery 50->60
# is tens of hours of waiting.
#
# Meditation is the intended way out: CChar::Skill_Meditation adds a mana point
# per stroke and the skill's DELAY is 2.0,1.0 seconds. This times it.
#
# (Note for the record: the OSI passive-meditation bonus in
# skills/skill46_meditation.scp is commented out at every call site, so the
# only meditation effect on this shard is the active one.)

wait_world
sleep 2000
ensure_peace
request_skills
sleep 2000
skill_report

# Drain the pool.
loop 5
  cast 6 self
  wait_action
  sleep 300
endloop
say drained

# Time the refill under meditation.
skill 46
wait_action
say meditating
wait_mana 20
say mana full

sleep 500
logout
