# M1 acceptance scenario — Revolution Offline.
#
# Proves the bot behaves as a real network player against Sphere Source-X:
# enter world, open the backpack, speak, walk 10 legal tiles, stay connected
# past the 10-minute mark, log out cleanly.
#
# Every line is a player action. No server state is touched directly.

wait_world

# Let the initial world burst land (0x78 mobiles, 0x1A items, 0x11 status,
# 0x4E/0x4F light, 0x20 draw-player) before acting.
sleep 3000

backpack
wait_backpack

say selam
sleep 1000

# 10 legal tiles: 5 east, then 5 back west. The first move in a new facing is
# a turn, which the server acks without moving; the step follows.
walk e 5
wait_walk
sleep 500
walk w 5
wait_walk

say selam

# Stability window: stay connected and idle. The 0x73 keepalive is the only
# traffic here, which is exactly what DeadSocketTime is about.
hold 600000

logout
