Mage / scribe start stats are STR 50, INT 25, DEX 5 (Professions.cpp `mage` and `scribe`). Owner: "mage ins can start with 50 str 25 int 5 dex that is normal". Do not treat STR 50 on casters as a bug.

**Why:** owner: casters take STR 50 at creation because STR is hard for a mage to farm later (no mining/melee in the build); INT/DEX they can raise. Sphere creation caps 80 total; INT 25 = 25 mana → circle 4 (11 mana) castable/scribable. Scribe was 50/20/10 and could never reach the Recall rung (Thalia OINT=10, 2026-09-04).

**How to apply:** other caster hybrids (warlock 50/10/20, tamer, alchemist, mage_blacksmith 50/20/10) NOT covered by this ruling — ask before changing. Pre-existing chars keep old stats; they need real stat gain (see [[scribe-int-gain]] once sphere-expert answers).
