The owner's governing design rule (2026-09-04): bots must not just "do a
profession"; each activity must produce the next (tamer recruits hunt ->
supplies from bank -> dungeon -> crystal -> PK ambush -> tailor robe ->
vendor -> PvP bot wears it at Brit bank). Catalogue, priorities, hubs,
TRAINING_MODE vs ACTION_MODE and micro-activities live in
`docs/LIVING_WORLD_ACTIVITIES.md`; sparring detail in
`docs/M13_BRIT_BANK_SPARRING.md`.

**Why:** forum reading — Revolution life clustered at Brit bank (spar,
trade, guild staging), Minoc, mage shop, stables, docks, dungeons, treasure
sites, houses, vendor rows.

**How to apply:** when scoping any bot goal, ask what it hands to the next
activity (item, party, rumour, order). Sequence: sparring chain (party
protocol is the gate) -> guild staging + dungeon party -> bank haggling ->
tamer/treasure -> duels/spectators -> boats/PK/housing. Head Hunters stay
"not needed yet" (re-confirmed 09-04); PK activity = ambush AI only. Related:
[[sparring-parties]], [[caster-str-via-wrestling]].
