Alchemist loop: buy a lot of nightshade + empty bottles from the NPC alchemist/mage shop, brew (poison potions first — cheapest reagent, sells), and let one batch serve both training and stock to sell. Train and sell are the same action, not two goals.

**Why:** Owner ruling 2026-09-04 after Elara/Selene opened their first session with `REPLACE_EQUIPMENT` buying 4 NPC heal potions and never touching Alchemy. An alchemist buying potions is backwards — she is the supplier.

**How to apply:** Upkeep must not outrank the profession's own craft for a crafter who can make the consumable; reagent/bottle buy quantities derive from gold + recipe (dynamic thresholds rule), not a fixed 4. Sell output player-first, then interim NPC (see [[interim-npc-sales]], [[craft-market-tiers]]).
