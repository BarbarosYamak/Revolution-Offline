No global disengage threshold. A fighter that always leaves at 40% HP will
never stand in a real PvP fight later. Retreat is a judgement: expected
incoming damage vs remaining HP, target count, whether the fight is
training-mode PvM or a duel/PK, bandage availability.

**Why:** owner rejected my "disengage ~40%" survival fix on 2026-09-04
(8 deaths / 0 kills wave). Survival must come from target choice (single
target, skill-tier hunting ground, no travel at critical HP), not from
cowardice baked into every fighter.

**How to apply:** survival briefs = pick better fights and stop overpull;
any retreat rule reads the fight context and the character (personality,
build, mode), never a constant. See [[stat-targets-and-variants]],
[[living-world-chains]] (TRAINING_MODE vs ACTION_MODE).
